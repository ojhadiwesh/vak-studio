# Mahāmṛtyuñjaya — sources and credits

## Text
Ṛgveda 7.59.12 (seer Vasiṣṭha Maitrāvaruṇi, deity Rudra as Tryambaka, metre anuṣṭubh); also in the Yajurveda
(Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā 3.60, Taittirīya Saṃhitā 1.8.6). *Oṃ* prefixed as in recitation. Written with the pāda break
spaced (बन्धनान् मृत्योर्) so captions split by pāda.

## The legend
Mārkaṇḍeya and Yama (Śiva as Kālāntaka, "ender of Death"): told in the Purāṇas (e.g. Śiva Purāṇa, Mārkaṇḍeya
tradition; temple tradition places it at Thirukkadaiyur). Versions differ (the mother's name, the boon's wording).
The link between Mārkaṇḍeya and this mantra is devotional tradition; the Ṛgveda credits the verse to Vasiṣṭha.
The narration says "the prayer that conquers death", not that he composed it. **Have a teacher review the narration.**
