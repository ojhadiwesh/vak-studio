# Mahāmṛtyuñjaya — the legend of Mārkaṇḍeya (storyboard)

Photoreal live-action style (`--style photoreal`). A narrated story (English) told as the legend; the **mantra is the
climax**: the boy's prayer, chanted as the rescue happens. Timings are estimates until we have the narration and mantra audio.

**Structure (≈ 90 s):** title card ~6 s → narrated legend ~53 s (15 shots, ~3.5 s each) → the mantra once, ~25 s (6 shots) → end.

**Mantra** (Ṛgveda 7.59.12):

> ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् ।  
> उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान् मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात् ॥

- ॐ — *Om.*
- त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे — *We worship the three-eyed one,*
- सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् — *fragrant, who makes all life flourish.*
- उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान् — *As the ripe cucumber is freed from its vine,*
- मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात् — *may we be freed from death, not from immortality.*

**Character:** Markandeya, a slim sixteen-year-old Indian boy with a shaven head and a short tuft of hair (shikha) at the back, three horizontal lines of white sacred ash (tripundra) on his forehead, a rudraksha bead necklace, a white sacred thread across his chest, a plain white cotton dhoti and a white cloth over one shoulder, bare feet, a calm devoted face.

N07 (worship) is the character anchor: generate it first (3 takes), approve it, then draw the other shots of the boy from it.

## Rules applied (from the chant-film-gpu skill)

- Every shot ≈ 3.5–4.3 s at real speed, one action, one camera move.
- Deities are never drawn: Śiva = light from the liṅga; Yama = buffalo hooves, a horned shadow, a noose.
- Photoreal faces morph most: faces in profile or from behind, "face stays steady" in every face shot; parents never face camera.
- Shots without people say "no people or hands appear"; hands-only shots (N04, N08) and wide/tiny-figure shots are drawn without the character reference.
- Plain frame edges; no loose fabric in empty scenes.
