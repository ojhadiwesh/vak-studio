Bold like a creator app.
Rooted like a chant.

One set of tokens for Vāk's web app, player and share cards. The interface is a modern creative tool: neutral surfaces, big media, a glowing gradient for actions. The Indian character lives in three places only: the brand gradient's pigments, the chant typography and a few thin motifs.

Principles

Five rules every screen follows.

1Media first

Films and frames are the colour. The interface stays neutral so they can shine.

2One glow

The gradient marks what creates or what's live: Generate, progress, the lit word. Never decoration.

3The chant leads

Devanagari is the hero type. Only the chant, the logo and word chips use it.

4Trust is visible

Every chant and film shows its provenance seal: Verified, Identified or Interpreted.

5Calm motion

Quick to respond, slow to settle. Playful on hover, never bouncy on sacred text.

Colour

Brand pigments come from Indian festival colours: genda (marigold), kesar (saffron), rani pink and neel. Neutrals carry 95% of the interface and swap with the mode.

Do and don't

Use the gradient for Generate, progress and the lit word.
Let films and frames carry the colour.
Show Devanagari large, with IAST and meaning beneath.
Gradient text on body copy or more than one gradient button per view.
Deity images, temple clip-art or ornament on controls.
Bouncy or spinning motion on the chant text.
