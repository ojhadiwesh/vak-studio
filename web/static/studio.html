What makes a Vāk film trustworthy.

Grounded in a source

Every chant carries a provenance badge. Identified texts cite their source; unidentified ones are labelled Interpreted and go to review.

No invented stories

Scenes come from what the words mean. A story is told only when a cited text tells it.

Open to a teacher's review

Translations and word meanings are drafts until a teacher checks them; AI-read chants are marked Interpreted and queued for review.
